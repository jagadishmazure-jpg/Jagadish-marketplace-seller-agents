# SOP-01 — Listing Standards

## Title requirements
Titles must be 20-140 characters, specific, and free of keyword stuffing.
Repeated words, ALL CAPS, and excessive punctuation lower the quality band
and may trigger a policy violation.

## Description requirements
Descriptions must be at least 40 characters and accurately describe the
item's material, size, and condition. Vague one-line descriptions are
flagged as needs-work.

## Images
Every listing should include at least one clear image. Listings without
images convert worse and score lower.

## Prohibited content
Counterfeit goods, keyword stuffing, and misleading claims
("best deal", "buy now" pressure tactics) are prohibited and scored zero
on compliance.
