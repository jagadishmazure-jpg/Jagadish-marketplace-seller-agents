# SOP-03 — Seller Performance Policy

## Tiers
Sellers are tiered platinum, gold, silver, bronze by rating and defect
rate. Tiers are recomputed from trailing performance, not lifetime totals.

## Defect rate
Defect rate above 0.10 triggers a suspension review (T4). Between 0.05
and 0.10 the seller is placed on a watch list (T3) with a remediation plan.

## Ratings
Sustained ratings below 4.0 require a listing-quality audit: the scorer
bands every active listing and the seller must fix poor-band listings
before new listings are approved.
