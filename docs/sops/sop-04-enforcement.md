# SOP-04 — Enforcement

## Suspensions
Seller suspensions require human confirmation through the confirmation
gate. The defect rate, tier, and open dispute count are attached to the
request so the approver sees the full picture.

## High-value refunds
Refunds above $500 require confirmation. The gate records who approved
and when, in the hash-chained audit log.

## Violations
Critical violations (counterfeit) immediately escalate to T4 and open a
trust-and-safety case. Major violations trigger a listing takedown
review. Minor violations generate a warning with a fix deadline.
