# SOP-02 — Dispute Handling

## Triage order
Disputes are triaged by the rules engine in evaluation order:
1. Counterfeit allegations go to trust-and-safety immediately.
2. Amounts over $500 are escalated; auto-actions are capped below that.
3. Item-not-received with no delivery scan is refunded.
4. Damaged items with replacement stock are replaced.
5. Everything else is escalated with a reason for human review.

## Evidence
Keep the delivery scan record and the buyer's messages attached to the
dispute. Decisions cite the rules that fired so they are auditable.

## Communication
Buyers receive the decision, the cited policy rule, and the next step.
Never promise a refund before the triage decision is recorded.
